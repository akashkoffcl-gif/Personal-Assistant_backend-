### 1. Foreground Microphone Service Protocol
- **Service Name:** `com.nova.companion.voice.NovaVoiceForegroundService`
- **Foreground Service Type:** `android:foregroundServiceType="microphone"`
- **Start Constraint:** Initiated strictly from user UI trigger.
- **Ongoing Notification:**
  - Channel: `nova_voice_service_channel` (Importance: `LOW`)
  - Icon: `ic_btn_speak_now`
  - Ongoing flag: `true` (Cannot be swiped away while recording)
  - Content Text: Dynamic reflection of `VoiceState` (`Listening...`, `Processing...`, `Speaking...`, `Ready`).
- **Stop Control:** Direct action button `Stop Voice` stops foreground service, unregisters microphone listeners, releases audio focus, and dismisses notification.
