# 63.2-05 — Foreground Microphone Service Physical Verification

**Project:** NOVA Personal AI Companion  
**Phase:** 63.2 — Fresh APK Build, Physical Installation & Native Voice Certification  
**Target Platform:** Android 16 (API 36)  

---

### 1. Android 14–16 Foreground Service Enforcement
- **Manifest Requirement:**
  ```xml
  <service
      android:name=".voice.NovaVoiceForegroundService"
      android:exported="false"
      android:foregroundServiceType="microphone" />
  ```
- **Runtime Constraint:** Android 14+ prohibits initiating a service with `foregroundServiceType="microphone"` from a background context without an ongoing user task or exempting condition.
- **Trigger Origin:** Service is started strictly via user-initiated UI action (`startVoiceForegroundService()` in bridge or notification actions).

---

### 2. Notification Channel & User Controls
- **Channel Name:** `NOVA Voice Intelligence Service` (`nova_voice_service_channel`)
- **Importance:** `NotificationManager.IMPORTANCE_LOW` (non-intrusive ongoing presence).
- **Persistent Notification UX:**
  - **Title:** `NOVA Voice Subsystem`
  - **Dynamic Status Text:** Displays real-time state (`"Listening for your command..."`, `"Processing intelligence query..."`, `"Speaking response..."`, `"Ready"`).
  - **Action Button:** One-tap `Stop Voice` (`ACTION_STOP_VOICE`).
- **Termination Verification:**
  - Clicking `Stop Voice` invokes `NovaVoiceForegroundService.stopService()`.
  - Executes `voiceActivationManager?.stopVoiceSession()`.
  - Executes `stopForeground(STOP_FOREGROUND_REMOVE)` and `stopSelf()`.
  - Audio hardware is released and notification is immediately dismissed from the notification shade.
