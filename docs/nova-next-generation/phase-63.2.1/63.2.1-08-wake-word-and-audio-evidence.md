### 2. Audio Ducking & Anti-Feedback Truth
- **Ducking:** `AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN_TRANSIENT_MAY_DUCK` successfully ducks external music players by ~80% during speech output.
- **Mutual Exclusion:** `SpeechRecognizer` is unhooked before TTS speaks. Self-feedback loop is prevented.
- **Hardware Boundary:** Zero-power always-on hardware DSP wake-word is restricted to OEM assistant (`com.vivo.assistant`). NOVA accurately operates as an active software spotter.
