## 1. Rate Limiting Mechanism

- **Infrastructure:** Redis-backed distributed rate limiting via `RedisService` with in-memory fallback.
- **Key Pattern:** `rate:heartbeat:<deviceId>`
- **TTL Window:** 5 seconds.
- **Policy:** Enforces a minimum 5-second interval between consecutive heartbeats per device ID. This permits normal 15-minute periodic heartbeats and startup pings while rejecting rapid denial-of-service or loop conditions.
