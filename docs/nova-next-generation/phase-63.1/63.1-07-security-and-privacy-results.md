# 63.1-07 — Audio Privacy, Security & Anti-Feedback Verification

**Project:** NOVA Personal AI Companion  
**Phase:** 63.1 — Native Voice Evidence Closure & Certification Audit  

---

### 1. Privacy & Audio Capture Boundary Audit
- **Zero Passive Background Recording:** The application never opens an audio recording stream without an explicit user session or active foreground service notification.
- **No Continuous Audio Upload:** Audio waveforms are never streamed to third-party endpoints or stored on NOVA servers. Only transcribed text is passed to the AI conversation endpoint.
- **No Raw Audio Persistence:** Neither `AudioRecord` buffers nor raw PCM/WAV files are written to disk or Room databases.
- **Credential & Secret Protection:** Log outputs strictly log endpoint status codes and error categories; Bearer JWT tokens and private keys are never printed in plaintext to logcat or console logs.

---

### 2. Audio Pipeline & Anti-Feedback Protections
- **Self-Feedback Prevention (Acoustic Echo Isolation):**
  - Before `VoiceSessionManager` opens the microphone, any active `NovaSpeechSynthesisManager` speech is immediately halted.
  - Before `NovaSpeechSynthesisManager` speaks, `SpeechRecognizer` is stopped and cancelled.
  - This strict mutual exclusion completely eliminates the assistant triggering or responding to its own vocal output.
- **Barge-In / TTS Interruption:**
  - Invoking `startVoiceSession()` or tapping the stop action instantly cancels ongoing speech synthesis via `TextToSpeech.stop()`.
- **Audio Focus Restoration:**
  - `NovaAudioFocusManager.abandonAudioFocus()` is invoked unconditionally in `onSpeechCompleted`, `onError`, and `onDestroy`, ensuring background media resumes normal volume levels.
