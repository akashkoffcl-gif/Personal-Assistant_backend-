## 1. Proactive Risk Vectors Evaluated

The intelligence engine scans user platform state to proactively flag operational risks:

1. **Overdue Task Warnings**: Compares pending task deadlines with the current timestamp and prioritizes urgent items.
2. **Upcoming Strategic Deadlines**: Identifies active projects with approaching milestones that have incomplete linked tasks.
3. **Cognitive Load & Focus Deficits**: Tracks completed focus session duration against the daily target (e.g. 120 minutes) and suggests restorative breaks.
4. **Routine & Habit Adherence**: Flags uncompleted daily habits before evening quiet hours.
5. **Financial Budget Deviations**: Detects when monthly spending trends exceed configured category limits.

## 2. Notification Delivery Intelligence

- **Quiet Hours Enforcement**: Proactive spoken audio is strictly suppressed between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM, routing alerts silently to the In-App Notification Center.
- **Deduplication**: Notifications are deduplicated to avoid generating repetitive warnings for the same overdue entity.
